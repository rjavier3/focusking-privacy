Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Welcome to Stop my procrastination. We believe your focus, tasks, thoughts, and emotional triggers are deeply personal. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to protecting your privacy.

1. 100% On-Device Storage

All data you enter into Stop my procrastination—including your focus tasks, subtasks, emotional reflection logs, mindful pledges, and session history—is stored exclusively on your local device. We do not transmit or store your personal data on external servers.

2. No Tracking or Data Selling

We do not collect advertising identifiers, track your activity across apps, or sell your personal information to data brokers or third-party marketing services.

3. No Account Required

You can use all core features, focus timers, procedural ambient soundscapes, emotional intervention triggers, and auto-journals without signing up or creating an account.

4. Data Control and Deletion

You have full ownership of your data at all times. You can delete tasks, clear individual reflections in your Auto Journal, or wipe all app data simply by clearing the application's storage in your device settings.
